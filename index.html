Our story

Built for people who start strong and fade fast

Thread exists because most to-do apps punish a single bad day with a reset to zero — and that's exactly the moment people give up for good.

Why we built this

Most of us don't fail because we lack ambition. We fail because the plan for tomorrow looks exactly like the plan for today: too long, too vague, and unforgiving the moment life gets in the way. Thread is built around a simpler idea — a list you can actually finish, and a second chance on the days you can't.

What we believe

  • A shorter, realistic list beats a long aspirational one, every time.
  • Missing a day shouldn't erase your progress — it should offer a way back.
  • Progress should be visible: a streak, a medal, a number that moves.
  • Your data is yours. We don't sell it, and we don't need to.

How it works

Onboarding sizes your daily list to your actual capacity instead of your ambition. Routines let you bundle recurring tasks — like a morning routine — on or off in one tap. Medals mark real milestones from day 10 to day 150. And if you miss a day, a recovery quest gives your streak one honest shot at survival instead of taking it from you outright.

Get in touch

Thread is a work in progress, built and refined based on what actually helps people stay consistent. If something isn't working for you, that's useful to know.
