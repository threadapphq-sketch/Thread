Privacy Policy

Last updated: [add date when you publish this]

This policy explains what information Thread collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Thread is a personal task and habit-consistency app.

Information we collect

  • Task and progress data — the tasks you add, your streak, medals earned, routines, and focus session history. This is stored to your account so the app can show your progress across visits.
  • Leaderboard information — if you use the Leaderboard, your chosen display name and your medal counts are shared publicly with other users of the app. No other personal data is included in this shared data.
  • Google account information — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google to display in the app. We do not receive your Google password or access to your other Google data.
  • Support chat — messages you send to the in-app support assistant are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. These conversations are not stored after you leave the page.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to advertisers or third parties.
  • We don't run ads or ad-tracking in the app.
  • We don't collect your data for purposes beyond running the app's features.

How your data is stored

Your task and progress data is stored per account and is not visible to other users, except for the specific fields noted above under "Leaderboard information." We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Thread is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can stop using Google Sign-In at any time by signing out in the app. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
